Prompt for designers/devs

Copy and adapt this prompt when briefing designers, developers, or generators.

You are designing and building a responsive marketing website for a new cloud provider called Island Computing Services (ICS). ICS is an Indian, developer-first cloud focused on simplicity and transparent pricing. Implement the specified navigation, structure, and UX, optimized for clarity and low friction.

Brand + general UX: clean, modern, minimal, easy to scan. Make actions visible and repeated: Try for free, Request demo, Contact. Fully responsive.

Global header: Logo linking home; nav items Products, Pricing, Resources, Company, Contact; right-side Sign in + Try for free; hamburger on mobile.

Home: hero, ask-anything box, Why ICS tiles, products overview, ecosystem links, narrative/credibility, pre-footer CTA, footer.

Products: /products hub plus detail pages for compute, database, storage with features, use cases, pricing teaser, and links to pricing anchors.

Pricing: transparent sections with #compute, #database, #storage tables, calculator with vCPUs/RAM/storage/egress, free credits, FAQ, CTA row.

Resources: hub linking to blog, docs, guides, community; include “Still need help?” pointing to contact.

Company: mission/vision, story, milestones, cards linking to About, Team, Careers, Press, Trust, Contact.

Utility/footer: status, legal pages, and consistent pre-footer CTA across pages. Maintain semantic HTML and accessible markup.
